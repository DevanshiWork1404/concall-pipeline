## TL;DR

- HDFC Bank reported quarterly PAT of Rs 19.5 billion, representing ~12.7% year-on-year growth excluding trading gains.
  > quarterly PAT of R s 19.5 billion, which is a growth of 12.7% approximately if you exclude the trading gains.
- Total AUM grew 15.3% YoY, driven by strong growth in SME and retail assets.
  > Total AUM grew 15.3% year - on - year, including a 1.9% impact of FCNR (B) leverage, with SME and retail assets leading the charge.
- Deposits grew 18.8% YoY on a reported basis, supported by a 4 percentage point lift from the special FCNR (B) window.
  > Deposits increased 18.8% year - on - year on a reported basis, including a 4 percentage point lift from special FCNR (B) window.
- Asset quality remained strong with GNPA at 1.11% (down 13 bps YoY) and credit costs low at 44 bps.
  > Our asset quality remains pristine, with GNPA at 1.11%, 13 basis points lower than year - on - year. Credit costs remain low at 44 basis points
- Deputy Managing Director Kaizad Bharucha requested early retirement by end of December, alongside ongoing MD & CEO transition to Anup Bagchi.
  > Kaizad, has requested for a n early retirement by end of December.

  > firstly, with respect to margins, again, margins are down to 3.32. ... One is, coming on the interest expense side, you'll see that our cost of funds is flat quarter - to - quarter, at about 4.4% last quarter to this quarter.
- **Q (Rikin Shah):** How are FCNR funds deployed and how will margins move in the coming quarters?
  **A:** FCNR funds are fully deployed into assets for ANBC and PSL compliance; floating rate loans (~70%) will benefit from rate hikes by December, driving an upward margin trajectory from Q3. ⚠ check numbers
  > how are you deploying the FCNR funds? ... The trajectory will be upward from the next quarter onwards.
