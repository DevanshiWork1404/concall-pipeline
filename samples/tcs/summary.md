# Tata Consultancy Services Limited - Q2 FY27

*NSE · call date: 2026-10-08 · TCS 532540 · [source PDF](https://nsearchives.nseindia.in/corporate/TCS_CORPCS_14102026155941_SEInt14102026_signed.pdf)*

## TL;DR

- TCS reported Q2 FY27 revenue of Rs 73,188 crore, representing constant currency growth of 0.5% QoQ and 2.8% YoY.
  > Revenue for the quarter was ₹73,188 crore . In constant currency, revenue grew 0.5% sequentially and 2.8% year-on-year .
- Annualized AI revenue reached $3.1 billion, crossing 10% of total company revenue.
  > Annualized AI revenue reached $3.1 billion and crossed 10% of our overall revenue.
- Quarterly TCV stood at $9.6 billion, excluding major strategic deals such as Porsche and Best Buy.
  > TCV for the quarter was US$9.6 billion . This excludes the Porsche and Best Buy deals.
- Operating margin remained flat sequentially at 24%, while net margin stood at 19%.
  > Our operating margins remained stable sequentially at 24% . ... Our net margins for the quarter were 19% .

## Management commentary

- TCS continued investing in strategic partnerships, M&A initiatives, and niche talent to build long-term capabilities.
  > In Q2, we made investments in strategic partnerships, M&A-related initiatives, and hiring of niche talent.
- Near-term skills and delivery needs were met by increasing subcontractor hiring and maintaining a higher bench capacity.
  > As we build future capacity and navigate demand transitions, we have invested in a higher bench and increased hiring of subcontractors to meet near-term skills and delivery requirements.
- LTM attrition in IT services decreased by 30 basis points sequentially to 13.3%, with total associates at 598,056.
  > taking our workforce to 598,056 associates. ... LTM attrition in IT services reduced by 30 basis points sequentially to 13.3% .
- Strategic engagements like Porsche/MHP acquisition and Best Buy GCC highlight TCS's focus on AI mobility and AI-native GCC transformations.
  > Our partnership with Porsche and the proposed acquisition of MHP , which is subject to regulatory approvals, strengthen our position in AI powered mobility and engineering. - The Best Buy GCC engagement highlights our ability to help clients build and transform AI-native global capability centers.

**Tone: positive** - Management emphasized steady sequential growth momentum in international markets, strong order book wins ($9.6 billion TCV), fast scaling AI revenues ($3.1 billion annualized), and broad-based vertical performance.

## Guidance / outlook

- TCS maintains its policy of not providing specific quarterly or annual revenue and earnings guidance.
  > As you are aware, we don't provide any specific revenue or earnings guidance
- Management expects MHP acquisition-related dilution to impact operating margins by approximately 50 basis points depending on timing and integration.
  > The acquisition-related dilution, particularly in MHP, is likely to impact by about 50 basis points depending on the timing of closure and when the integration starts.
- Over the medium term, management remains confident that revenue growth momentum will continue based on TCV, deal pipeline, and client discussions.
  > on a medium-term basis, looking at the TCV, looking at the pipeline and the client conversations that we are having, we're confident that growth momentum will continue.

## Segment performance

- **BFSI:** Strongest large vertical; grew 2.5% QoQ and 3.9% YoY in constant currency, supported by banking, capital markets, and insurance.
  > BFSI was our strongest large vertical and the largest contributor to growth, with 2.5% sequential and 3.9% year-on-year growth in constant currency .
- **Manufacturing:** Grew 3.1% QoQ and 4.2% YoY in constant currency, led by factory automation, industrial AI, and supply chain projects.
  > Manufacturing grew 3.1% sequentially and 4.2% year-on-year . Demand is improving around factory automation, engineering productivity
- **Technology and Services:** Grew 3.1% QoQ and 4.8% YoY in constant currency, driven by enterprise software, cloud modernization, and engineering.
  > Technology and Services grew 3.1% sequentially and 4.8% year-on-year . Growth was driven by enterprise software, technology products, engineering
- **Consumer Business:** Declined 0.7% QoQ and 1% YoY in constant currency due to inflation sensitivity and discretionary spending cuts.
  > Consumer Business declined by 0.7% sequentially and 1% year-on-year . Retail and consumer products remain sensitive to inflation, energy costs, and selective consumer spending.
- **Regional Markets / India:** India revenue grew 6% YoY and Regional Markets grew 3.6% YoY, though India experienced sequential revenue pressure from a project deferral.
  > India revenue grew 6%, and Regional Markets grew 3.6% year-on-year. ... In India, we had a project deferral, which caused this revenue decrease.
- **International Markets:** Grew 1.2% QoQ in constant currency across all regions, led by UK at 3.5%, followed by North America and Continental Europe at 0.4% each.
  > International revenue grew 1.2% sequentially in constant currency. All international markets grew sequentially , led by the United Kingdom at 3.5%, followed by North America and Continental Europe at 0.4% each.

## Risks / concerns

- Continued selectivity and scrutiny on discretionary spend programs without immediate business value.
  > The demand environment has not materially changed since last quarter, and discretionary programs without near-term value remain under scrutiny.
- Margin headwinds in H2 FY27 from MHP acquisition dilution (~50 bps), seasonal furloughs, and elevated ecosystem investments.
  > The acquisition-related dilution, particularly in MHP, is likely to impact by about 50 basis points depending on the timing of closure and when the integration starts. The normal furlough seasonality and our continued ecosystem investments would be our headwinds.
